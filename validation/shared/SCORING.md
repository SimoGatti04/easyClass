# Shared scoring conventions

## Qualitative 0–4
- 0 — unusable / incorrect
- 1 — major correction required
- 2 — usable with meaningful defects
- 3 — good, minor defects
- 4 — excellent / nearly no correction

## Teacher editing effort
- usable_as_is
- minor_edits
- moderate_edits
- major_rewrite
- unusable
